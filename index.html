Mary Wollstonecraft Shelley - Frankenstein

Welcome to the mini digital edition of Mary Shelley's Frankenstein. Made as a project for Text as Data II, taught at the University of Antwerp.

In the Text as Data classes, we discussed what digital scholarly editing is and learned about various digital methods, tools, technologies, and programming languages that can be used to create a digital scholarly edition. Digital scholarly editions are usually based on texts, such as transcriptions of notebooks, drafts or manuscripts, annotated by the scholarly editor.

The story of how the novel Frankenstein came to be, has since become an infamous story of its own. The first science-fiction novel came into existence during an unnaturally cold and dreary summer, in a villa in the Swiss Alps. It has since then become a worldwide cultural phenomenon with numerous adaptations, interpretations and studies.
The work touches upon themes of scientific positivism, the relentless progress of the industrial age, responsability, insanity and religion. All concepts that capture the zeitgeist of the time it was written, the beginning of the 19-th Century and its anxiety about the rapid progress and changing way of life.
